## Findings

- Best model: MobileNetV2 transfer head (ImageNet backbone frozen, 10 head-training epochs on GPU). Test accuracy 0.8207, macro-F1 0.7425, 2,422,597 parameters, ~17 ms/image on GPU.
- Most confused pair: crack is the weakest class (F1 0.37 on only 20 test images; 137 total), often confused with dent/scratch - few samples and visual similarity. dent recall is 0.66 (some dents predicted as scratch or major_body_damage).
- broken_glass and major_body_damage are best-recalled (F1 0.93 each). Grad-CAM heatmaps are generated per prediction (src/gradcam.py) to verify the model attends to the damaged region; spot-check samples in notebook 04.

## Limitations observed

- crack is severely under-represented (137 images total; 20 in the test set), so its F1 is unreliable and should improve with more data. Trained CPU-only with a frozen backbone (no fine-tuning) due to no GPU; more epochs/fine-tuning/Colab GPU would likely raise accuracy. Severity is rule-based, not a learned label - state this assumption in the report. Repair-cost ranges and the 300 sample claims are synthetic/indicative (INR), not real insurer data.
